                    SHOPIFY CHECKOUT EXTENSIBILITY - PHASE 2

1. SHOPIFY FUNCTIONS (Backend Logic & Rules)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Shopify Functions execute high-performance backend logic in WebAssembly directly 
on Shopify’s infrastructure.

- Discount Allocators:
  Build custom discount rules (e.g., complex tier discounts, custom BOGO logic,
  volume pricing, customer-specific discounts).

- Cart Transform:
  Manage product bundling, line-item price overrides, kit expansion, and component
  pricing natively in the cart and checkout.

- Fulfillment Constraints / Order Routing:
  Apply custom business rules to dictate shipping origin locations and fulfillment
  routing constraints.


2. CHECKOUT UI EXTENSIONS (In-Checkout Visuals & Interactivity)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UI extensions render custom UI elements inside pre-defined targets throughout 
the checkout funnel.

- Custom Banners & Notices:
  Display targeted warnings, announcements, shipping threshold progress bars
  (e.g., "Add $10 more for free shipping"), and compliance notices.

- Custom Input Fields:
  Capture custom order attributes and metafields (e.g., gift messages, delivery
  date pickers, delivery instructions, tax IDs, engraving text).

- In-Checkout Upsells & Cross-sells:
  Offer complementary add-on products or warranties directly within the checkout
  summary or order steps.

- Trust Badges & Custom Content:
  Render security seals, policy guarantees, custom refund info, or buyer verification
  notices.

- Conditional Interactivity & Modals:
  Trigger popups/modals for age verification, terms & conditions acknowledgment,
  or custom address validations.


3. POST-PURCHASE UI EXTENSIONS (Post-Payment Page)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Appears after payment is processed but before the Thank You page.

- One-Click Upsells / Downsells:
  Offer additional products that customers can purchase with a single click without
  re-entering payment or shipping info.

- Attribution & NPS Surveys:
  Collect buyer insights (e.g., "How did you hear about us?", rating scales,
  feedback forms).

- Post-Purchase Promo Codes & Offers:
  Provide time-sensitive discount codes or claimable free gifts for future orders.

- Educational Content & Next Steps:
  Display onboarding steps, digital download access instructions, or product care
  guidelines.


4. CUSTOMER ACCOUNT UI EXTENSIONS (Order Status & Customer Portal)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Extends New Customer Accounts pages and the Order Status (Thank You / Tracking) page.

- Order Status Page (Thank You / Tracking):
  Add custom order tracking links, re-order buttons, digital asset download buttons,
  and support widgets.

- Customer Account Dashboard:
  Add custom tabs, widgets, and info cards inside the buyer's account profile and
  order details pages.

- Self-Service Order Management:
  Allow customers to edit order details (e.g., update shipping address before
  fulfillment, request order cancellation, or initiate returns).


5. BRANDING API (Styling & Design System)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Programmatically customize the global look and feel of Checkout and New Customer 
Accounts to match brand identity.

- Color Palette & Schemes:
  Control primary accents, background colors, surface colors, text colors, and
  alert state colors.

- Typography & Fonts:
  Custom font families, size scales, font weights, and line heights.

- Logo & Header Placement:
  Custom logo positioning, alignment, sizing, header backgrounds, and favicon
  configuration.

- Component Styling Controls:
  Set custom border radii, button padding, input field borders, focus ring styles,
  and card shadows.

- Layout & Dividers:
  Customize header/footer structure, section spacing, corner radii, and container
  divider lines.
